Wow this game is bad, 6/10

Before playing this game, I noticed how everyone online seemed to absolutely love. It has reviews of 9/10 everywhere. The first time playing it, I got to the screen where it asks you to pick the difficulty. I'm all about hard games, so my first instinct is to pick the hard difficulty. This game offers you three levels of difficulty to pick from: Easy, Normal, and Hard. But if you pick hard, you have a limited number of saves, and the game does not autosave. So iI started my playthrough after picking hard, and after the intro cutscene, the first gameplay section begins.

The first thing I notice is that the controls feel very stiff. The animation for Leon running around looked really weird, kind of like he was waddling around or had something stuck in his pants. It just did not feel good to play. ANds

The controls and combat feel stiff and clunky. There is no dodge, there is no quick turn around, and the running is slow.

The second playthrough of the game you can play as a new character, but 95% of the game stays the same. But the games doesn't tell you this. So after playing Leon's campain, I played Claire's expecting something different. Any enjoyment I had playing the Raccoon Police Department the first time went out the window the second time I played it. The same puzzles, the same enviroment

Wow this game is bad, 6/10
